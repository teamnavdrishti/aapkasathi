# Aapka Sathi

> **Har Waqt, Har Pal, Ab Aapke Saath.**

Aapka Sathi is a **voice-based citizen assistance platform** that helps people access government information through a simple phone call.

The project is designed especially for people who may find modern digital applications difficult to use. Instead of requiring a smartphone, app, or complex interface, citizens can simply **make a phone call and ask their questions using their voice**.

## 🎯 Problem

A large number of citizens face difficulties accessing government information because:

- They may not be comfortable using websites or mobile applications.
- Important government information is spread across different platforms.
- Finding the correct scheme or service can be confusing.
- Elderly citizens may prefer speaking rather than typing.
- Many people are more comfortable communicating in Hindi or Hinglish.

This creates a gap between citizens and the digital government services available to them.

---

## 💡 Solution

**Aapka Sathi** provides a simple voice-first solution.

A citizen can make a phone call and ask a question such as:

> "Kisanon ke liye kaunsi government scheme available hai?"

The voice agent understands the question, searches the relevant knowledge base, and provides the available information through a voice response.

No complicated application interface is required for the basic interaction.
